Privacy Policy

SonicNotes is designed to record and organize audio on your device.

Information We Collect

SonicNotes does not require you to create an account. The app may store audio recordings, notes, tags, and recording settings on your device. If iCloud Drive is enabled, your recordings may be stored in your personal iCloud Drive according to your Apple account settings.

Microphone Access

SonicNotes requests microphone access only to record audio. Audio recordings are created when you choose to start recording.

How We Use Information

Your recordings, notes, and tags are used only to provide the app’s recording, playback, organization, and sharing features.

Data Sharing

SonicNotes does not sell your data. SonicNotes does not share your recordings, notes, or tags with third parties. If you choose to share or export a recording, that action is controlled by you.

Purchases

SonicNotes may offer in-app purchases. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. SonicNotes does not receive or store your payment card information.
