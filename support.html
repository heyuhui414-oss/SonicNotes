Common Questions

Where are my recordings stored?

SonicNotes stores recordings on your device. If iCloud Drive is available and enabled, recordings may be stored in your personal iCloud Drive.

Why does SonicNotes need microphone access?

Microphone access is required only for recording audio.

Can I share my recordings?

Yes. You can share recordings from the recording library or detail screen.

Does SonicNotes collect my recordings?

No. SonicNotes does not upload, sell, or share your recordings.
